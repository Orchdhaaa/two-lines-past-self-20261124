Two lines to my past self: a warning about one specific moment, and one sentence on why it mattered. The program writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), 234 characters, plain and vivid, no wasted words.

> Don't put off the biopsy the doctor orders on Tuesday; go that week, not in four months.
> Waiting turned a twenty-minute surgery into a year of chemo, and that was the year your daughter learned to walk while you lay in a hospital bed.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

The entry is written to `/output/entry.txt` by default; set `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` to write it elsewhere.
